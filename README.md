# Island Gridiron GM

A Hawaiʻi high school football GM game that runs in the browser. Call plays on Friday night, recruit from every island, chase a state title, and play a friend online.

## Play

Open the site link for this repository (Settings → Pages shows it). Everything runs in your browser:

- **Careers** save automatically in the browser you play in. Use *Load game → Export file* to keep a backup or move a career to another device.
- **Band music**: on Game Day, add songs from your own device. They stay in your browser and are never uploaded.
- **Play a friend online**: choose *Host*, send your friend the page link and the four-letter code, and they choose *Join*. No account needed. The two browsers connect directly (WebRTC via the free PeerJS service). Some school or work networks block that kind of connection; home Wi-Fi and phone data usually work.

All players are fictional.
